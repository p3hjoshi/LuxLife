About & privacy

Life OS is a browser-based productivity dashboard. It stores your entries locally using this browser's storage. It does not sync automatically, connect to an AI service, or block other apps. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to access its local data. Avoid entering sensitive financial or personal information.

Tip: On iPhone, use Safari's Share menu → Add to Home Screen for an app-like shortcut. Browser storage and background timer behaviour can vary by iOS version.
